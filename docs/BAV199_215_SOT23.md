## Manufacturer pin mapping (top view)
| Pad | Terminal | Clamp use in Measurement_board_v_1 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A1: anode of diode 1 | GNDA |
| 2 | K2: cathode of diode 2 | +3.3VA |
| 3 | K1/A2: series diode midpoint | SUM_U / SUM_V / SUM_W |

Current direction through series diodes is **pin 1 → pin 3 → pin 2**. These are not common-anode or common-cathode diodes.

## Circuit-design warning
BAV199 is a low-leakage signal clamp, **not an energy-absorbing TVS**. With +3.3VA absent it can back-power the 3.3 V rail through the upper diode. Verify clamp currents, available source impedance, startup/shutdown behavior and overcurrent fault response before validating the final schematic. The clamp must never replace the inverter's independent hardware overcurrent protection.
