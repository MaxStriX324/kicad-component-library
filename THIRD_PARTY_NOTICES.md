# Third-party CAD models

The UCC21750 symbol and SOIC footprint in this repository were drawn independently from public mechanical and pin-assignment data. They are **not** redistribution of the user-supplied SnapEDA/SnapMagic CAD export.

The STEP models from third-party packages have **not** been uploaded. Check applicable redistribution rights before publishing them.

The EC3503 WRL represents only an approximate envelope; pin numbering is provisional, and its dimensions must be checked against a physical sample before manufacturing.
